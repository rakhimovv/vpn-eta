# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about the code, not the person.

## What that means here

Expected:

- Take the other person's problem seriously, even when the cause turns out to be their
  keyboard layout or their SwiftBar version.
- Give feedback about the change, not its author.
- Accept that "no, that is out of scope" is a legitimate answer, and that maintainers of a
  spare-time project owe nobody a response on a schedule.

Not accepted:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery; unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information — including a colleague's or employer's VPN
  hostnames, usernames or infrastructure — without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages and any other space representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Report anything that needs attention through GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting form](https://github.com/rakhimovv/vpn-eta/security/advisories/new),
which reaches the maintainer directly and is not public. Reports are handled privately and the
reporter's identity is not disclosed.

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues or pull requests, and block accounts
for behaviour that does not fit the above. Sanctions are proportionate; a first mistake made
in good faith is normally a conversation, not a ban.

This is a condensed policy in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is worth reading if you want the longer treatment.
